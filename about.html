Our Story

VividVista International (VIYO) manufactures and supplies premium hotel & resort room amenities, RFID locks, minibars, safes, kettle & tray sets, bath accessories, housekeeping trolleys, and lobby fixtures across India.

Our team works to deliver reliable, eco-conscious and intelligently designed products that enhance guest comfort, operational efficiency and long-term value for our partners — every item is chosen for how it performs under real hospitality conditions.

We serve hotels, airports, corporates, IT parks, government and architects nationwide, with pan-India delivery, on-site warranty, and responsive after-sales support.
